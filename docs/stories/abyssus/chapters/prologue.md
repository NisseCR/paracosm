You open your eyes, to see not the light of the sun, but that of tundra in shadow, nature in grey. A cold wind blows and anchors you in the moment, as you try and make sense of your surroundings. Of this place in time preserved.

Before you—some fifty meters in the distance and across an all-surrounding chasm—sits a monolith that looms over you, marked by a singular slit of a gate that reveals a darkness within. A gate carved not by the hands of giants, but of titans.

{% audio "wind" %}

_This is the tower_, you think to yourself.

The thought comes to you as if you remember where you are. But you don’t. All of this feels familiar, yet you find yourself to be a mere observer of the moment all the same.

_The tower..._

Looking up only reveals a mist that engulfs this place, and a fading silhouette of something enormous beyond. It seems endless. Daunting.

_Timeless..._

A sense of vertigo overwhelms you. You crouch down and clasp the frozen moss beneath your feet to steady yourself, as you look forward once more. Towards that gate.

The darkness is calling to you. A whisper reaches your ear, from across the chasm and through the pervasive howling wind. It tells you—without words—that it’s waiting for you, staring at you, from within. It is watching the very next step you make. But it is wary, for the whisper is not an invite.

It is a warning.
